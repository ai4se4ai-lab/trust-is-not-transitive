# Decisions

- Preserve the provided IEEE template originals; use its class/options and IEEEtran bibliography style.
- Treat all learned guards, human approval and tool effects as untrusted unless
  their interfaces and assumptions are discharged by the runtime.
- Correct T2: intersection/minimum/product bounds apply to veto ensembles;
  disjoint early-accept routes require a sum of joint route losses. Pairwise
  correlation alone does not determine higher-order intersections.
- Correct T3: threshold the conditional risk reduction per incremental cost;
  a threshold on an arbitrary classifier score is not generally optimal.
- Distinguish expected conformal risk control from high-probability risk
  certification. Never call the first a per-calibration high-probability bound.
- Lightweight simulation is an admissible feasibility substitute under the prompt;
  it cannot establish effectiveness on real LLMs or named external benchmarks.
