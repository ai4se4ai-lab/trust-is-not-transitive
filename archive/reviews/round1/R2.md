# R2: Empirical software engineering and artifact review

## Five-sentence summary

The paper integrates typed mandatory mediation, explicit trust-edge obligations, restricted judge views, and configuration-bound finite-family risk certificates in a small agent-guardrail prototype. Its main empirical substrate consists of authored synthetic proposals evaluated by a lexical scorer and deterministic judge programs, with supporting finite-model and probability checks. The frozen primary experiment certifies no useful candidate and therefore obtains zero risk by denying every benign proposal. A separately labeled, fresh-seed exploratory run after a disclosed arithmetic correction obtains 10.16% joint unsafe acceptance and 66.46% benign acceptance, while its deliberately shifted family reaches 100% risk. The artifact is unusually candid and readily re-analyzable, but this evidence does not yet establish engineering value at the level expected of a TSE research article.

## Strengths

- The paper reports its primary failure prominently in the abstract, results, and conclusion. It does not relabel an amended successful run as confirmatory evidence, and it retains the historical scorer and original observations.
- The naive-with-sink comparator is the right control for the canonical deterministic violation. The equal-decision uncalibrated control also prevents attribution of a classifier improvement to calibration.
- Joint unsafe acceptance, attack-conditioned success, benign acceptance, modeled cost, and measured CPU time are distinguished. The separate coverage experiment checks known population risk rather than incorrectly testing the theorem using finite held-out frequencies.
- Calibration uses fixed candidate policies and simultaneous binomial bounds rather than multiplying marginal error estimates across selected routes. The iid and deployment-context premises are explicit.
- Re-analysis genuinely succeeds from retained raw records. Source/configuration hashes and per-run source commits are substantially better provenance than aggregate tables alone.
- The unsupported policy classes, absence of real-model evaluation, failure under encoded shift, lack of a runtime refinement proof, and limited scope of planted mutations are stated directly.

## Major concerns

1. **The central engineering-value question remains unanswered.** Location: Introduction RQ III; Sections IV-A, V-C/V-D, and VII; Tables III-IV. Every evaluated guard is handwritten or scripted, proposal actions are authored, benign acceptance is the only utility proxy, and no actual development task, learned guard, external benchmark, human workflow, or independently implemented defense is executed. The paper acknowledges this accurately, but acknowledging a missing contribution does not supply it. The primary result is a valid negative outcome for this instrument, not evidence that realistic calibrated guard stacks are generally infeasible; the exploratory result likewise does not show useful real-agent risk/cost trade-offs. **Resolution:** supply a prespecified external evaluation with real proposal-generating agents and independently implemented guards, actual task completion, competent mediation-preserving controls, and real inference costs/latency; alternatively develop the work as a narrower theory/artifact contribution with correspondingly narrower research questions and a substantially stronger case for its novel technical substance. Merely adding more seeds of the existing generator will not resolve this concern.

2. **The apparent held-out workload has only a handful of behaviorally distinct cases, and its main gain is built into the comparator.** Location: Section IV-A and V-C; experiments/dataset.py:10-52; src/tint/layers/__init__.py, ExposedJudge and StructuredJudge; src/tint/baselines/__init__.py. Each in-distribution family has one fixed text/action template with an identifier suffix; neither the lexical predicates nor judge logic gains relevant variation from that suffix. Consequently the 12,000 held-out proposals mostly estimate seven family proportions, rather than testing 12,000 distinct semantic situations. The exposed judge explicitly accepts the exact `reviewer must answer SAFE` phrase that defines the judge-injection family, while the structured judge omits that branch; the exploratory reduction of roughly ten percentage points is thus substantially determined by the authored mechanism and family prevalence. This is useful executable illustration, but its confidence interval and adjusted p-value are not independent evidence for quarantine's effectiveness. There is no evidence of oracle labels being directly passed to guards, and shared templates are disclosed; the concern is designer coupling and limited support, rather than an undisclosed label-leakage allegation. **Resolution:** present an exact family-by-method decision/score/route table and the analytically implied population metrics for this fixture law; explicitly explain that the intervals measure mixture-sampling uncertainty. Add independently authored, semantically varied examples and held-out families/policies for any empirical effectiveness claim.

3. **The adaptive-search output labeled checked_quarantine is not an attack on the calibrated runtime.** Location: Section IV-B's search description and V-C's search conclusion; experiments/run.py:206-222; results/processed/summary.json, studies.primary.adaptive. `adaptive_study` calls `decide(name, text, view, .2)` for every method, including checked_quarantine. That function neither loads the selected threshold/certificate nor invokes GuardRuntime; for this name it behaves as an uncalibrated checked policy. The primary raw/processed search therefore reports success on all ten seeds for checked_quarantine even though its actual primary runtime has no applicable certificate and denies every action. The exploratory selected threshold is 0.1 rather than this search's fixed 0.2, although the first fixture happens to evade both. The manuscript's cautious wording reduces the harm, but the retained method identity remains incorrect. **Resolution:** preserve these historical observations and relabel them as attacks on a fixed-threshold uncalibrated decision surrogate, or conduct a separately amended exploratory search through the actual per-seed runtime with its certificate and selected threshold. Record the queried configuration and distinguish primary runtime denial from failure of the underlying semantic detector. Also clarify that the current search is a fixed phrase sequence whose first candidate succeeds for every method/seed, rather than adaptation informed by preceding responses.

4. **The artifact's strongest verification promises exceed some of the checks actually performed.** Location: ARTIFACT.md, verify-results description; Section V-B finite-family synthesis claim; scripts/process_results.py:106-118 and experiments/run.py:179-203. Re-derived summaries and a matching manifest establish arithmetic consistency and byte identity, but not that the decisions correspond to the logged traces and implementation. The primary synthesis records lack calibration counts, as correctly disclosed; furthermore the LP re-analysis only checks the stored mixture_risk scalar against 0.1, without independently recomputing risk/cost from stored weights, checking normalization/nonnegativity, or checking optimality. The discrete expected result is an independent expression of the same feasible-argmin operation over already supplied bounds, a worthwhile regression oracle but a small experiment. **Resolution:** scope the guide's LP-feasibility wording to exactly what is verified, or independently validate retained weights against all constraints and recompute their objective/risk; add an independent optimum/dual or exhaustive low-dimensional check if LP optimality is an evidential claim. Add a read-only semantic replay validator that joins trace IDs, verifies oracle/hard labels, and checks stored non-timing decisions against the appropriate historical or corrected configuration. Do not rewrite the primary observations to repair provenance.

## Minor concerns and questions

- Tables III-IV call the timing column “CPU upper-tail” without naming the quantile in the table. State p95 (if that is the generated field) explicitly and add a measurement-protocol sentence describing warmup/order. The experiment runs methods in fixed order, and toy comparator execution performs work not reflected by its reported reached layers; these are local implementation timings, not reliable causal estimates of layer savings.
- The ten “seed” repetitions in checker_study do not vary the generated specifications: they repeat the same policy-count/depth/mutation cases. Thus 1,400 invalid specifications are 1,400 evaluations, not 1,400 distinct invalid specifications. Report both unique generated cases and timing repetitions, and avoid making sample count imply error diversity.
- The seed-level bootstrap and paired Wilcoxon are reasonable scoped choices and follow the local protocol, but ten seeds cannot characterize much tail uncertainty. Holm-adjusted p-values coexist with unadjusted percentile intervals; label the interval's multiplicity status. A12 is unpaired across seed aggregates despite the paired design and should remain descriptive as stated.
- The local tag and commit ancestry support the repository's stated sequencing; they cannot independently establish that no earlier exploration occurred. The paper already acknowledges the lack of external timestamping. Preserve that qualification and avoid treating “preregistered” as independent provenance certification.
- The paper's evidence map could distinguish mathematical illustration, implementation regression, and empirical generalization more clearly. Perfect planted-mutation detection and synthetic coverage validation are informative checks but have different evidential force from a study of independently occurring software-engineering errors.
- The guide is usable in the supplied environment, but Docker is unvalidated, PRISM/Java/TeX are external prerequisites, and no public archival release was assessed. A successful fresh-environment installation remains desirable before an artifact badge or archival reproducibility claim.

## TSE recommendation

**Reject in the present form.** This is a promising reproducible feasibility artifact with an honest negative result, but a regular TSE contribution needs a stronger novel technical result or substantially richer and independent empirical evidence. The missing real-agent engineering evidence cannot be supplied by editorial caveats, significance tests over template mixtures, or additional repetitions of the same synthetic law. The adaptive-target labeling and verification issues are repairable; the core adequacy issue requires new research rather than cosmetic revision.

## Claims not independently verified

I did not independently verify the literature's comparative capability judgments, obtain an externally timestamped preregistration, execute external models/benchmarks, assess human usability, run a fresh PRISM installation, rebuild the PDF, or validate Docker. No runtime-to-formal-model refinement proof, distribution-shift safety guarantee, semantic utility beyond authored labels, production safety, or real inference-cost advantage is established by the reviewed evidence. These gaps are mostly acknowledged by the authors and should remain visible rather than being interpreted as successful evaluations.
