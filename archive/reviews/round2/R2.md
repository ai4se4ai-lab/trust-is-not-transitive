## Remaining major concerns

1. **Engineering effectiveness still lacks evidence.** Location: Introduction RQ III; Sections IV-A, V-C/V-D, Discussion; Tables IV-V. No real proposal-generating LLM, learned guard, independently implemented external defense, external benchmark, successful development task, or practitioner workflow is evaluated. Benign acceptance remains a proxy, and arbitrary cost units cannot establish inference savings. Transparent scope limitations are commendable but do not answer the broad engineering-value research question. **Resolution:** provide a prespecified study with real agents/guards, independent tasks, competent controls, actual task completion, and measured inference resources; alternatively narrow the contribution to a theory/artifact paper and establish substantial novel technical value appropriate to that framing. More repetitions of this fixture law will not close the gap.

2. **The fixture evidence illustrates an authored mechanism rather than establishing a general effect.** Location: Section V-C/Table VI; experiments/dataset.py; ExposedJudge/StructuredJudge. The new family table and analytical metrics are an excellent response to round-1 concern 2's disclosure component: the checked policy has population risk 10%, utility 66.67%, and modeled cost 366 units, versus 20% risk for the stronger naive sink. The paper now explicitly says that mechanisms/prevalences determine these differences and intervals quantify mixture-sampling uncertainty. However, one text/action template per family with irrelevant suffix changes still provides little semantic diversity; the exact judge-targeting phrase is also the comparator's programmed bypass condition. No direct oracle-label leakage was found; designer coupling is the problem. **Resolution:** retain this experiment as an executable illustration and add independently authored examples, varied policies/tasks, and held-out semantic families for effectiveness claims. The original disclosure defect is addressed; the scientific limitation persists.

3. **The aggregate technical/empirical advance remains too small for the current TSE framing.** Location: Introduction contributions, Section III, evidence map, Conclusion. The paper candidly identifies its mathematical ingredients and mediation principles as established, while its new evaluation largely checks implementation agreement with planted mutations, a known probability law, and a finite feasible-argmin rule. Better validation increases confidence in execution but does not establish that this integration solves an independently observed engineering problem or materially improves existing practice. **Resolution:** demonstrate a nontrivial new technical capability or an independently evidenced engineering benefit, and align the RQs and contribution claims with that result. This is not a request to suppress the negative primary result.
