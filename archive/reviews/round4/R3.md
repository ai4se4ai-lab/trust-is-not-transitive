2. **Practical SE value and sufficient incremental novelty remain missing.**
   **Location:** RQ III, Section V-C/Tables III–V/Figure 3, Discussion/Conclusion.
   **Why:** The primary certified policy denies all benign fixtures; exploratory calibration does not change decisions relative to its uncalibrated control. No development task is completed or patch correctness assessed, and there is no maintained-defense comparison or actual inference-cost evaluation. Compare task success at matched safety/utility points and measure actual costs. Alternatively pursue a narrower negative-feasibility/assurance-method contribution with an appropriate publication scope.

3. **End-to-end provenance and executor correspondence are assumed at the difficult boundary.**
   **Location:** Definition 1 and Section III-D/obligation table; `src/tint/model/__init__.py`, `src/tint/agents/__init__.py`.

   **Resolution:** Specify field/output dependency semantics, exercise multi-step derivation and transmission through an adapter, test omitted references and secret-bearing targets/arguments, and provide a bounded refinement or adapter assurance argument.

4. **Static workload certification does not provide adaptive attacker protection.**
   **Location:** Threat model, Section III-B, Section IV-A, Section V-C shift/search discussion.

The 20% target, 25.40% unsafe fraction among accepted exploratory proposals, and 100% encoded-family acceptance emphasize that difference.
