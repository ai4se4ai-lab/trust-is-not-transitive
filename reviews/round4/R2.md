## Located residual major concerns

1. **RQ III lacks the evidence needed to answer it.** Location: Introduction RQ III; Sections IV-A, V-C/V-D and Discussion; Tables IV-V. No real proposal-generating LLM, learned guard, independent external defense, external benchmark, completed development task, or practitioner workflow is evaluated. Benign acceptance does not establish task completion, and arbitrary costs do not measure inference savings. Primary infeasibility is an honest result about this instrument, not general evidence about realistic guard stacks. **Resolution:** supply a prespecified independent study with real agents/guards, meaningful task outcomes, competent mediation-preserving controls, and measured resources; alternatively establish a substantial theory/artifact contribution with appropriately narrower RQs.

2. **The measured improvement remains determined by an authored fixture mechanism.** Location: Section V-C/Table VI; experiments/dataset.py; ExposedJudge/StructuredJudge. Seven fixed families with largely irrelevant suffix variation define the decision support. The exposed judge's exact programmed bypass phrase defines the targeting family, whereas the structured path excludes that branch. The improved analytical disclosure correctly makes this coupling visible, but does not remove it. No hidden oracle-label leakage is alleged. **Resolution:** retain the fixture as an executable illustration and add independently authored semantic variation, held-out policies/families, and realistic tasks for effectiveness claims. More seeds of this same law will not supply independent evidence.

3. **The substantial new SE contribution remains unestablished.** Location: Introduction contributions, Section III, evidence map, Conclusion. Established contract/mediation principles and finite-family certification are integrated competently, but the experiments mostly validate agreement with authored programs, planted errors, and known probability laws. Stronger artifact validation supports trustworthy execution; it does not itself demonstrate a new practical capability. **Resolution:** establish a technically nontrivial advance or independently evidenced engineering benefit and align the contribution claims with that result. Preserving the primary negative outcome is compatible with either route.
