## Remaining major concerns

1. **Substantial novelty is still not demonstrated.** Locations: introduction and RQs (`main.tex:68–126`), Theorems 0–4, related work, discussion. I have no remaining identified substantive mathematical correctness objection to the scoped results. However, their ingredients are standard conditional reasoning, complete mediation, elementary probability identities, binomial correction, and Lagrangian sufficient optimality. Since the paper explicitly disclaims mathematical novelty, the distinctive contribution must reside in an integration benefit. Neither the theorem suite nor the existing controls demonstrates what this trust-edge discipline enables beyond conventional mandatory mediation with version-bound calibrated policy selection. **Resolution:** identify and evaluate a concrete distinctive executable capability, or reposition the work as a narrower feasibility/experience contribution. Further wording repairs alone cannot supply the missing advance.

2. **Formal validation remains weak evidence about the implementation.** Locations: operational-state description and correspondence table; `main.tex:515–521, 629–634`; `formal/cascade.pm:4–17`; `formal/check.py:8–33`. The PRISM model directly encodes six canonical routing configurations and their outcomes; it does not model the important certificate, authenticated approval, provenance, or edge-evidence mechanisms. The independent Boolean check defines execution to exclude the forbidden predicate and checks that exclusion. The manuscript correctly labels these as finite sanity/counterexample checks and admits the absence of refinement, so this is an evidential limitation rather than an undisclosed correctness defect. Nevertheless, the paper's formal-methods case provides little independent assurance beyond inspection and tests. **Resolution:** add implementation-level verification, a checked semantic correspondence, or independent cross-component fault validation. A refinement proof is one possible remedy, not an unconditional requirement for every engineering contribution.

3. **The engineering-value question remains unanswered by the fixture law.** Locations: evaluation design, family-support table/population calculations (`main.tex:765–791`), shift/search discussion, discussion/limitations. Within-family behavior is scripted, and observed improvements largely estimate known mixture proportions. The calibrated and uncalibrated checked policies still make identical corrected decisions; the primary certified policy still denies all benign tasks; the shifted family still fails completely. These observations validate fallback/accounting mechanics but cannot establish that the assumptions hold in realistic software-agent work or that the integration improves actual task utility. **Resolution:** narrow the research questions and contribution to mechanics/feasibility, or add independently specified tasks and learned guards with matched integration controls. Additional sampling from the same authored law would not close this gap.
