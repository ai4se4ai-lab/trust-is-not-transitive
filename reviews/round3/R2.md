## Remaining major concerns

1. **RQ III remains empirically unanswered.** Location: Introduction RQ III; Sections IV-A, V-C/V-D and Discussion; Tables IV-V. There is no real proposal-generating model, learned guard, independent external defense, external benchmark, completed development task, or practitioner workflow. Benign acceptance is not task completion; arbitrary layer costs are not inference-resource measurements. The primary failure is informative about this instrument, but cannot establish general infeasibility or practical value of realistic guard stacks. **Resolution:** perform a prespecified independent evaluation with real agents/guards, meaningful task outcomes, competent mediation-preserving controls, and measured inference resources; alternatively narrow the paper to a theory/artifact contribution with sufficient novel technical substance. Additional seeds of the same generator will not resolve this issue.

2. **The principal measured effect remains authored into a tiny fixture law.** Location: Section V-C/Table VI; experiments/dataset.py; ExposedJudge and StructuredJudge. Seven fixed text/action families, varied mainly by irrelevant identifier suffixes, determine essentially all decisions. The deliberately exposed judge accepts precisely the targeting phrase appearing in the corresponding family, whereas the structured path omits that branch. Analytical risk differences and sampling intervals therefore characterize this construction rather than demonstrate quarantine effectiveness on independent semantic cases. This limitation is now accurately disclosed; I do not allege hidden oracle-label leakage. **Resolution:** retain the fixture as an executable illustration, and add independently authored semantic variation, held-out policies/families, and realistic tasks before making empirical effectiveness claims.

3. **A substantial new SE contribution is still unestablished.** Location: Introduction contributions, Section III, evidence map and Conclusion. Established mediation/contract principles, finite-family certification, and a small integration may be useful, but the supplied experiments mostly validate agreement with authored programs, planted errors, and known probability laws. Repairing artifact verification establishes trustworthy execution rather than novel practical capability. **Resolution:** demonstrate a technically nontrivial new capability or an independently observed engineering benefit and align the broad research questions with it. This concern does not require replacing or concealing the primary negative result.
